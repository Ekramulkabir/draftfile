1Site Overview

Strengths & Opportunities

🏆
Seclusion Within an Active Employment CorridorThe site sits directly across from several major technology headquarters, including Google's Palo Alto offices and VMware, and is within minutes of Stanford Research Park, appealing to high-income renters who value an easy commute while avoiding the noise and density typically associated with office districts.
👥
Strong Public School DistrictAssigned schools include Barron Park Elementary, Ellen Fletcher Middle School, and Henry M. Gunn High School, all highly rated public schools and a meaningful draw for older professionals and households with children.
🍂
Opportunity for PlacemakingThe vacancy of the Miranda Avenue site creates a clean-slate development opportunity for deliberate decisions about architecture, amenities, landscaping, and community programming tailored to the target renter profile.

Challenges & Considerations

⚠
Limited WalkabilityThe site sits in an auto-oriented office park environment with minimal pedestrian infrastructure and no walkable retail or dining in the immediate vicinity.
▮▮
Unproven Rental Market ContextMiranda Avenue does not have an established rental residential identity, so the development will need to build its own reputation and sense of place.

Two complementary residential programs
  • Recommend two residential components with distinct programming to capture different market segments.
  • Low-Rise: Four acres of low-density apartments totaling ~120 units.
  • Standard Luxury: Phased development of ~800 luxury apartments on the northwest portion of the site.
3Positioning & Renter Profile

Typology / Positioning

◎
Recommend a mixed low-rise and Standard Luxury program emphasizing seclusion, school-district appeal, green space, and proximity to employment.

Renter Profile

Low-Rise

Audience:
Established Professionals, Families, and Empty Nesters & Downsizers

Financial Status:
$250,000–$500,000+

Motivation:
Safety, privacy, green space, larger floor plans, low-maintenance living

Standard Luxury

Audience:
Young Professionals, Established Professionals, and Empty Nesters & Downsizers

Financial Status:
$150,000–$350,000+

Motivation:
Proximity to employment, modern amenities, convenience, and green space

Positioning Rationale

◎
The site is positioned to appeal to the Palo Alto employee base seeking proximity to employment in a quieter, lower-density residential setting. Low-Rise units emphasize space and privacy, while Standard Luxury units provide a lower monthly entry point.

🤝 TIMING / PARTNERSHIPS

  • Near- to Medium-Term Opportunity: Strong multifamily fundamentals and vacant space support development at Miranda, though active leases and ongoing marketing may require phased delivery.
  • Early Partnership Encouraged: Engage development partners early, likely during the pre-entitlement stage; a JV would allow Stanford to maintain control and upside, while a long-term ground lease can provide stable cash flow and execution risk transfer.
